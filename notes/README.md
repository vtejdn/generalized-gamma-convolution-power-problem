# GGC research notes

This directory is reserved for notes that directly serve the active
generalized-gamma-convolution power problem.

- [classes-and-power-closure.md](classes-and-power-closure.md) records the
  exact HCM/GGC/SD/ID implications and safe power-closure statements.
- [bondesson-2015-product-closure-audit.md](bondesson-2015-product-closure-audit.md)
  reconstructs Bondesson's 2015 article, separates its proved product theorem
  from Conjecture 1, and extracts reusable proof mechanisms.
- [bondesson-1992-ggc-hcm-foundational-infrastructure.md](bondesson-1992-ggc-hcm-foundational-infrastructure.md)
  is a theorem-level audit of the foundational monograph. It records the
  Loewner and hyperbolic Stieltjes criteria, the GGC/HCM recognition
  machinery, the actual \(PF_\infty\) power induction, and the Bondesson-class
  intermediate target.
- [sato-id-volterra-size-bias-ggc.md](sato-id-volterra-size-bias-ggc.md)
  gives the measure-level source for the Volterra equation and derives, with
  explicit attribution, the finite-mean equivalence between GGC membership
  and a mixture-of-exponentials residual in the size-biased decomposition.
- [stable-mellin-poisson-tilt-infrastructure.md](stable-mellin-poisson-tilt-infrastructure.md)
  audits Zolotarev and the relevant parts of Bertoin, and records stable
  Mellin formulas, tilted-stable semigroup and gamma-product lemmas, and a
  tempered-stable Poisson construction of the negative tilt.

The complete theorem and obstruction record is the
[project ledger](../ledger/README.md), with stable identifiers collected in
the [WIP master index](../WIP.md). The current status authority is
[ResearchStatus.md](../ResearchStatus.md).
